Archive description

Stool samples from 112 individuals (two villages) in two rural communities of north-eastern Madagascar. Samples were collected by the Madagascar Health and Environmental Research (MAHERY) initiative between 2013 and 2014, and sequenced with Illumina HiSeq 2500.
