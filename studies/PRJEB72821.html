Archive description

The Christie NHSFT / University of Manchester have conducted a prospective study to collect stool and saliva samples from patients with advanced / metastatic biliary tract cancers having first-line chemotherapy. We plan to perform shotgun metagenomic sequencing of extracted faecal DNA, to determine species, strain and functional compositions of gut microbiomes before- and on-treatment. This data is part of a pre-publication release. For information on the proper use of pre-publication data shared by the Wellcome Trust Sanger Institute (including details of any publication moratoria), please see http://www.sanger.ac.uk/datasharing/
