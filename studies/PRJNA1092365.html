Archive description

Human fecal samples from hydatid patients, liver cirrhosis patients, and healthy controls (shortgun). Paired oral and fecal samples from patients with hydatid disease and cirrhosis (full-length 16S). Transcriptomes of S. anginosus cultures and liver tissues from Echinococcus-infected mice (bulk RNA-seq).
