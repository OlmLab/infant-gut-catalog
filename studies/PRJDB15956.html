Archive description

This is shotgun metagenome sequencing in the gut of Parkinson's disease patients. The sample size is 73 for healthy controls and 94 for Parkinson's disease. All studies were approved by the Ethical Review Committees of the Nagoya University Graduate School of Medicine (approval #2016-0151), Iwate Medical College Hospital (H28-123), Okayama Kyokuto Hospital (approval #kyoIR-2016002), and Fukuoka Medical College (approval #2016M027). We obtained written informed consent from all PD patients and healthy controls.
