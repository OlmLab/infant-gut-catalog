Archive description

Metagenomes were generated from DNA isolated from oropharyngeal swabs, stool samples, rectal swabs and vaginal secretions to determine effects of sequencing depth on alpha diversity and to determine the ratio of human to microbial reads seen when sequenced via shotgun sequencing.
