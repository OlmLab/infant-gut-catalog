Archive description

Shotgun metagenomic sequencing of tongue surface swabs and fecal samples from 105 patients with coronary atherosclerosis (AS), chronic coronary syndrome (CCS), and acute coronary syndrome (ACS). Illumina NovaSeq 6000, 150-bp paired-end. The dataset supports a two-stage study evaluating the incremental restratification value of oral and gut microbiomes in the clinical gray zone of coronary artery disease.
