Archive description

We performed shotgun metagenomic sequencing of fecal samples from 53 chronic functional constipation patients and 48 healthy controls to jointly profile bacterial, fungal, and viral community structure, functional modules, cross-kingdom correlation networks, and the diagnostic performance of multi-kingdom microbial signatures using machine-learning models.
