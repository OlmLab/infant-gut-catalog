Archive description

Bacterial metabolites derived from the gut microbiota can modulate key metabolic pathways, thereby influencing tumor growth and progression, which contributes to the alleviation of colorectal cancer. These metabolites play a crucial role in shaping the metabolic environment of the tumor, offering potential therapeutic strategies for managing colorectal cancer
