Archive description

To characterizate the gut microbiota (both bacteriome and virome) and the oral bacteriome of HIV-infected patients, to investigate their relationship with their inflammatory state and cardiovascular risk and to analyze the effects of the current integrase strand transfer inhibitors (INSTIs)
