Archive description

Previous iron supplementation studies in children have suggested adverse shifts in the gut microbiota. Given the importance of iron during early childhood it is essential to test whether food-based approaches have a similar negative impact. Using shotgun metagenomics, we examined the effects of 9 months' daily consumption of iron/zinc-biofortified pearl millet (FeZnPM) on the gut microbiome, compared to control pearl millet (CPM), in children living in urban slums of Mumbai, India as part of a randomized controlled feeding trial (Clinical Trials.gov ID: NCT02233764).
