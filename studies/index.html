Studies (2,886)

Every study in the catalog — an INSDC BioProject deposited in ENA, NCBI SRA or DDBJ and enumerated through ENA's mirror; human gut shotgun metagenomes of all ages — with the number of curated samples, the age-category mix, the most frequent health-condition code, the routes that contributed values (depth: R1 archive attributes · R2 supplementary tables · R3 paper full text · R4 abstracts) and the per-field coverage. Click an accession for the study page (how it entered the registry, papers, authors, coverage, evidence, sequencing, sample table, downloads). Gbp/run is the mean base count per run in Gbp from gut_runs.parquet. The table is loaded from studies_index.json; the full study table with every column is gut_studies.parquet.
