Studies (2,886)

Every study in the catalog — an INSDC BioProject (ENA, NCBI SRA or DDBJ) of human gut shotgun metagenomes, all ages — with its curated samples, age-category mix, most frequent health condition and the interventions the study administered (FMT, probiotic, diet …). Click an accession for the study page (evidence, coverage per field, papers, sequencing, sample table, downloads). Gbp / sample is the median sequencing depth per sample (bases summed over each sample's runs, gut_runs.parquet). The table is loaded from studies_index.json; the full study table with every column is gut_studies.parquet.
