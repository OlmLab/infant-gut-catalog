Archive description

Through shotgun metagenomic sequencing of fecal samples from participants with and without HIV from Botswana, Uganda and the US, we define shared and geography-specific gut microbiome profiles associated with HIV infection and antiretroviral treatment. We delineate changes linked to CD4+ lymphopenia, systemic inflammation and specific antiretroviral medications, and explore the mechanistic background to treatment-associated microbiome alterations.
