Archive description

Patients with cirrhosis and portal hypertension were prospectively enrolled prior to initiating lactulose. Patients had no antibiotic or alcohol use in the prior month. At baseline and at 4 weeks, all patients underwent the animal naming test (ANT) and fecal samples for metagenomic and 16S rRNA gene amplicon sequencing and targeted metabolite analysis.
