Archive description

BAL and stool was collected over the first two years of life from infants with cystic fibrosis (CF) in Melbourne, Australia who were treated with ongoing prophylactic amoxicillin/clavulanate to assess microbial maturation over this period. Low biomass BAL samples underwent 16S amplicon sequencing using custom primers for the V1-V2 region (F-27/R-338) and the Illumina MiSeq system. Stool samples underwent Illumina shotgun sequencing via the NovaSeq 6000 system.
