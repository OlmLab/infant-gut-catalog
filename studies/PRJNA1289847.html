Archive description

Bacterial communities of patient fecal samples profiled by shotgun metagenomics. Samples for both Melanoma (treated with anti-PD-1 plus anti-CTLA-4 therapy) and NSCLC (treated with anti-PD-1) patients were collected at several time points, including before fecal microbiota transplant (FMT) from healthy donors, and post-FMT (i.e., one week, one month, two months, three months, six months, and one year).
