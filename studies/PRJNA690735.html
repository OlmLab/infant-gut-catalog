Archive description

The putative loss of fiber fermenting-microbes populating the IBD gut could lead to dietary fibers not being efficiently broken down into their beneficial biproducts (e.g., short chain fatty acids SCFA), resulting in binding of intact fibers to pro-inflammatory host cell receptors.
