Archive description

Shotgun metagenomic sequencing data from a single-group design trial analyzing changes in the gut microbiome, stool quality, and gastrointestinal well-being in 15 healthy volunteers after a synbiotic intervention comprising Lactobacillus rhamnosus, LGG, Lactobacillus acidophilus, LA-5, Lactobacillus paracasei subsp. paracasei, L. CASEI 431, Bifidobacterium animalis subsp. lactis, BB-12 and 15 g of inulin consumed daily for four weeks. Fecal samples were collected at baseline and completion of intervention. The data is depleted of human sequencing reads.
