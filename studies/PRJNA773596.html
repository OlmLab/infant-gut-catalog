Archive description

Osteoporosis is a systemic bone disease which is prone to fracture due to the decline of bone mineral density and bone quality, the destruction of bone microstructure and the increase of bone fragility. In recent years, studies have reported that osteoporosis is related to the changes of gut microbiota. Therefore, RCT method was used to explore the relationship between osteoporosis and gut microbiota.
