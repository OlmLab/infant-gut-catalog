Archive description

Study of the impact of oral fluoropyrimidine chemotherapy on the gut microbiome in colorectal cancer patients, mice, humanized mice, and stool-derived in vitro communities. All experiments are described in the manuscript "Expansion of a bacterial operon during cancer treatment ameliorates drug toxicity."
