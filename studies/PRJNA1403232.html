Archive description

This study aims to comprehensively analyze the effects of factors such as sleep duration, sleep pattern, and social jet lag on adolescent obesity and changes in glucose and lipid metabolism, and to explore the role of gut microbiota in the relationship between sleep and obesity and glucose and lipid metabolism. The goal is to provide new insights and evidence for the prevention and intervention of obesity and glucose and lipid metabolic diseases in adolescents.
