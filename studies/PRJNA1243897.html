Archive description

Gut microbiome of people consuming a sourdough-leavened croissant enriched with fiber (4.8 g/100 g, croissant enriched with dietary fibers) for 2 weeks. The control group consumed a control croissant with 1.3 g/100 g.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of adult by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.70.
