Archive description

Antibiotics (Abx) are critical in modern medicine but can induce intestinal dysbiosis, exacerbate inflammatory bowel diseases (IBD), and disrupt the gut microbiome. Moreover, Abx treatment was reported as a significant risk factor for developing IBD, yet the underlying mechanisms remain unknown.
