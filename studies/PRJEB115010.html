Archive description

The AGMP ADRC Study is a targeted study that will recruit, assess, and longitudinally follow approximately 1000 participants already enrolled in an ADRC, for multiple years. Participants include cognitively normal “healthy controls” who may be at risk for Alzheimer’s disease, individuals with mild cognitive impairment (MCI), and individuals with Alzheimer’s Disease (AD). Most of these participants will have excellent characterization with neuropsychological testing, brain imaging, and a consensus-based confirmed diagnosis.
