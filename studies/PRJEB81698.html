Archive description

This study aimed to investigate whether 12 weeks of exercise training can revert and/or minimize the deleterious cardiometabolic effects of 4 weeks of carbohydrate overfeeding in individuals born with low birth weight and increased risk of developing type 2 diabetes. This study recruited a total of 48 healthy Caucasian males, born between 1979 and 1980 at full term (gestational weeks 39–41)
