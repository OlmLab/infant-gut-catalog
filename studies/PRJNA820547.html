Archive description

Raw metagenomic sequencing data of 143 people living with HIV (PLHIV). The included PLHIV were on long-term anti-virus treatment (median 6.35 years) and were virally suppressed (plasma HIV-RNA < 200 copies/mL). All participants are Caucasian individuals recruited from the HIV clinic of the Radboud University Medical Center between December 2015 and February 2017.
