Archive description

In this study, we investigated the effects of metformin on the transcriptome data of whole blood samples and the metagenome data of the gut microbiota in newly diagnosed type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM) patients. Our objective was to identify potential mechanisms by which metformin treats T2DM.
