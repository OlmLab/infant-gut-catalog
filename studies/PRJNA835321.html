Archive description

The present work studies the impact of maternal postpartum antibiotic prophylaxis on Infant gut and breastmilk microbiota in order to highlight the importance of judicious use of antibiotics in the postpartum period. This would result in a more balance infant gut microbiome and reduce the threat of antimicrobial resistance.
