Archive description

This study goal is to describe the landscape of gut microbiome in Thai primary liver cancer, particularly HCC and iCCA patients, compared to healthy controls. The study subjects are from the Thailand Initiative in Genomics and Expression Research for Liver Cancer [TIGER-LC]
