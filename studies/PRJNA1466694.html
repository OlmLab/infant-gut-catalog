Archive description

This study investigated gut microbiome and metabolomic profiles in adolescent depression using shotgun metagenomics and untargeted metabolomics. Depressed adolescents showed altered gut microbiota composition, disrupted lipid and folate metabolism pathways, and potential diagnostic metabolites. These findings reveal microbiota-gut-brain axis changes and provide new biomarker candidates for adolescent depression.
