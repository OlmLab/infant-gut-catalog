Archive description

The human gut microbiota is increasingly recognized as a key factor influencing inflammatory diseases and treatment outcomes. However, whether microbial signatures are unique or shared across the stability of disease-specific microbial signatures across closely related rheumatic conditions remains unclear. In this study, we compared the gut microbiome of treatment-naive patients diagnosed with rheumatoid arthritis and spondyloarthritis.
