Archive description

To provide an unbiased survey of the human gut virome, we performed an amplification-independent analysis on fecal VLPs using NGS (Illumina) sequencing(vNGS) and TGS(Pacbio) sequencing(vTGS). This project also includes un-enriched metagenome sequencing(mNGS).
