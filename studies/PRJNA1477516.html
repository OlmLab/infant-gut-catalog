Archive description

This project adapts Metagenomic Immunoglobulin Sequencing (MIg-Seq) for low-biomass infant fecal samples to investigate diet-microbiome-immune interactions. We applied this optimized protocol to 32 longitudinal samples from 16 infants enrolled in the MINT trial, a four-arm randomized controlled trial comparing meat-based, dairy-based, plant-based, and reference complementary feeding patterns. Fecal sampling was performed at 6 and 12 months (pre- and post-intervention) to capture the dynamics of the early-life gut microbiota.
