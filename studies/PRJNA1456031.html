Archive description

This study uses ex vivo incubation of human fecal samples to study the effects of commonly occurring food additives or contaminants on the gut microbiome. Samples were incubated for 48 hours and include untreated starting inoculum, untreated controls and treated samples. Community composition is assessed using shotgun metagenomic sequencing.
