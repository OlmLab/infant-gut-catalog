Archive description

Bulk metagenome (mNGS) and VLP-enriched (vNGS) sequencing data of healthy Chinese volunteers. The mNGS and vNGS samples were paired. The data were used in the study of "Enrichment culture but not metagenomic sequencing identified a highly prevalent phage infecting Lactiplantibacillus plantarum in human feces"
