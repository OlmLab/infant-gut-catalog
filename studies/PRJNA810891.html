Archive description

Patterns of microbial dysbiosis can be detected in fecal samples of GI-aGVHD patients peri-aGVHD onset. Markers of microbial health pre-GVHD onset are associated with longer survival and lower risk of GVHD-related mortality after allo-HCT.
