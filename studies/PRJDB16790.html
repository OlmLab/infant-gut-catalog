Archive description

Total microbial community DNA was isolated from Japanese fecal samples at Jikei University Kashiwa Hospital and Kobe University Hospital. This study population contains abdominal aortic aneurysm (AAA) patients and controls with coronary risk factors, such as hypertension, diabetes mellitus, or dyslipidemia, but without present or history of coronary or other vascular diseases.
