Archive description

Infection with human papilloma virus (HPV) type 16, is a risk factor for head and neck squamous cell carcinomas (HNSCC). In this study, we report on a pilot-sized study of the fecal microbiome of head and neck cancer patients before injection of therapeutic vaccine.
