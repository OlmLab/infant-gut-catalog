Archive description

Eight germ-free mice were inoculated with a single human stool sample. Eight weeks after inoculation, mice were sacrificed and luminal contents were collected from the duodenum, jejunum, ileum, cecum, and colon of each mouse and shotgun sequenced along with the human stool sample (the inoculum).
