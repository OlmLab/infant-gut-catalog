Archive description

This study contains shotgun metagenomic sequencing data from fecal samples of individuals with Irritable Bowel Syndrome (IBS) and healthy controls. The dataset aims to characterize taxonomic and functional differences in the gut microbiome associated with IBS and its subtypes.
