Archive description

Numerous studies have linked shifts in the gut microbiome composition to infection events in various hematological malignances including acute lymphoblastic leukemia (ALL). In order to get a view of the gut microbiome of pediatriac ALL patients we completed both 16S rRNA gene sequencing and metagenomic shotgun sequencing from individuals with ALL being treated at the IWK. Metadata that goes along with these samples will be uploaded with the final publication of our findings on this cohort.
