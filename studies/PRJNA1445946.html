Archive description

This BioProject contains host-depleted metagenomic sequencing reads generated from diverse human clinical specimens, including bronchoalveolar lavage fluid, throat swabs, blood, cervical swabs, and stool, together with matched process controls. The study was designed as a strictly controlled within-sample comparison of three library preparation workflows: DNA-based libraries, RNA-based libraries, and total nucleic acid libraries. The goal is to evaluate how library chemistry influences microbial detection and overall signal composition across different clinical sample types, including recovery of DNA and RNA pathogens and interpretation of metagenomic sequencing results. These data provide a benchmark resource for methodological comparison and for the development, validation, and interpretation of clinical metagenomic sequencing workflows
