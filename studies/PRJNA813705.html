Archive description

Evaluation of gut microbiota composition and functional potential in patients with acute myeloid leukemia at diagnostic and comparison to healthy matched volunteers. MicroAML clinical study. Study design available on ClinicalTrials.gov (NCT03881826). Data published and discussed in Potgens et al, Haematologica 2024. 16S and metagenomics raw data are shared.
