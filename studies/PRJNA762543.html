Archive description

We conducted shotgun metagenomic sequencing of fecal and saliva samples to characterize the gut and oral microbial communities of 62 healthy male Singaporeans, including 32 young subjects and 30 elderly subjects.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool / oral cavity shotgun DNA metagenome of adult and elderly by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.78. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model claude-sonnet-5 · health context: healthy male Singaporeans, young vs elderly comparison · population flags: healthy_volunteers. Centre City University of Hong Kong, first public 2021-12-18. Curated source gut_all_v1.
