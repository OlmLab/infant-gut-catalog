Archive description

This study provides raw metagenomic sequencing data (FASTQ format) of fecal samples collected from hypertension patients recruited in Daqing (temperate region) and Haikou (tropical region) of China. The dataset aims to support research on regional differences in gut microbiome composition and function among hypertension patients, and can be reused for microbiome-metabolome association analysis, comparative genomics, and epidemiological studies related to cardiovascular diseases and gut microbiota.
