Archive description

Longitudinal metagenomic sequencing data for a proof-of-principle study of Fecal Microbiota Transplantation to alleviate intestinal colonization by extended-spectrum beta lactamase (ESBL) producing Enterobacteriaceae. For a detailed sample description, see Singh et al, 2018 (PMID: 29566738).
