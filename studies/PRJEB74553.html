Archive description

This project contains all raw reads for the Metcalfe-Roach et. al (2024) papers describing the gut microbiome of people with and without Parkinson's disease in a Vancouver cohort. Shotgun metagenomic sequencing was performed on the Illumina NovaSeq platform (2x150 bp), generating ~3.5 million reads per sample.
