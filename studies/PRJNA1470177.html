Archive description

Gut microbiome analysis done on 37 patients with recently diagnosed Type 2 diabetes mellitus were randomized into 3 groups with interventions such as Group A Mustadi Ghana Vati, Group B Varadi Ghana Vati and Group C Metformin. The stool sample for Gut Microbiome were collected during baseline and 24 weeks after the intervention and analysed
