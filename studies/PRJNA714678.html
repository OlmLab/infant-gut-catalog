Archive description

Patterns of diurnal activity differ substantially between individuals, with early risers and late sleepers being extreme examples of circadian phase referred to as chronotypes. Growing evidence suggests that the late chronotype significantly impacts the risk of developing mood disorders, obesity, diabetes, and other chronic diseases. Despite the vast potential of utilizing chronotype information for precision medicine, the factors that shape chronotypes remain poorly understood. Here, we determined whether chronotypes are associated with different gut microbiome composition.
