Archive description

Cardiac surgery-associated acute kidney injury (CSA_AKI) may be related to the increased morbility and mortality in intensive care unit (ICU) patients. The bidirectional crosstalks suggest the important links between CSA_ AKI and dysbiosis of gut microbiota, so the aim of this study was to further investigate the possible connections during the process of CSA_AKI.
