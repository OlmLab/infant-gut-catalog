Archive description

Type 2 diabetes mellitus(T2DM) is closely associated with atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease. In this study, the gut microbiota and its metabolites were used to analyze the possible mechanism of coronary artery disease (CAD) in T2DM, and to establish a predictive model for CAD in T2DM patients.
