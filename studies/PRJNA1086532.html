Archive description

There is growing evidence that microbiome dysbiosis among neonates is associated with serious complications, including necrotizing enterocolitis and sepsis. This study characterizes the intestinal microbiomes of premature neonates with bacteremia by performing whole metagenome sequencing and analysis on weekly stool samples collected from 40 cases and 39 matched controls.
