Archive description

Background: Phospholipase A2 receptor (PLA2R)-positive membranous nephropathy represents the predominant antigen-specific autoimmune subtype of adult membranous nephropathy. Accumulating evidence indicates that gut microbiota contribute to the progression of renal diseases by modulating host immunity, preserving intestinal mucosal barrier homeostasis, and regulating metabolic microenvironments. Nevertheless, direct evidence regarding whether PLA2R positivity in renal tissues correlates with distinct gut microecological signatures in membranous nephropathy remains lacking.
