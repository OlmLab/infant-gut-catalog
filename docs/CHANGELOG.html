1.10.0 — 2026-09-28 (release R2026.9, all-age catalog site)

  • Site rebuilt around the all-age catalog. Every section — Studies (one page per catalog study), Cohorts, Samples (explorer), Fields, Authors, Scope, Methods, Downloads, Contribute, Changes — is now generated from the gut_* catalog tables and the registry_* tables; the former infant-only sections are gone. Study pages show the registry classification with its evidence, papers (registry links ∪ curated infant links), authors, age / condition / country / sex mix, per-field coverage by route, the cohort-wide statements applied to the study, evidence examples, a sample table and per-study downloads (data/studies/<PRJ>.csv.gz|.parquet, <PRJ>_determinations.csv.gz). Cohorts are recomputed at every release: curated cohort records plus registry papers that describe 2–4 catalog studies (or cite 2–4 studies all submitted by the same centre). The contribution worklist covers every catalog study with ≥ 50 samples that is below 50 % coverage on ≥ 3 of the five core fields.
  • Fix — duplicate cohort-wide rows. 1.9.0 shipped both an R3 (full-text) and an R4 (abstract) row for 58,659 sample × field pairs where a cohort-wide statement existed on both routes; gut_sample_determinations now keeps one statement per study × field (route precedence R1 > R2 > R3 > R4, then confidence): 1,787,854 → 1,729,195 rows. gut_sample_metadata_wide already carried the winning value and is unchanged.
  • README intro rewritten from the gut_* / registry_* tables (docs/package_readme_intro.md); the former infant description is kept under Infant extension (historical description). Release notes gain a catalog and a registry section. No new curation in this release.

1.9.0 — 2026-09-28 (release R2026.8, gut_all route R3 — full-text cohort statements)

  • Route R3 for the non-infant gut studies. For each of the 1,317 non-infant gut studies with an open-access paper, the first PMC full text was fetched from Europe PMC (1,262 studies attempted, 1,138 with full-text XML; 124 PMCIDs returned no XML); Methods → abstract → Results (one chunk ≤ 36 k characters, shorter where the token budget required) were read by two independent Sonnet-class replicates. A cohort-wide statement is committed only when both replicates agree and its ≤ 12-word quote is a verbatim substring of the text (confidence 0.7; 0.6 from Results); deterministic guards drop age ranges that span life-stage categories, antibiotic exclusion windows < 1 month, healthy_control without healthy/control wording, cohort-level disease codes in case–control designs, and ethics-committee-only country mentions. Result: 1,871 study-level statements over 789 studies (country 596, life stage 366, health condition 315 + detail 274, antibiotic exposure 241, sex 77, age 2) — see data/inputs/gut/r3/ in catalog-pipeline (merged determinations, unreplicated and subgroup side tables, per-study summary, token costs).
  • Effect on gut_sample_metadata_wide (all 579,252 samples): antibiotic exposure 7.2 % → 12.4 %, health condition 28.8 % → 30.9 %, sex 26.8 % → 28.6 %, country 89.2 % → 90.1 %; age_category = unknown 222,970 → 212,487 (R3 life stage now precedes the R4 abstract life stage; basis r3_fulltext_life_stage). Precedence per sample × field: infant catalog > R1 > R2 > R3 > R4; R3 rows are scope = study_all expanded to the study's samples that lack a higher-precedence value.
  • Limits: one paper per study (first PMCID; re-analysis papers yield nothing); 55 studies of one shard were not read (the extraction run for that shard was stopped by the model provider's content filter and was not retried); 362 studies were read with truncated chunks (< 16 k characters) to fit the per-worker budget; case–control group statements (2,754) are recorded but not assigned to samples. Infant tables unchanged.

1.8.0 — 2026-09-28 (release R2026.7, curated scope gut_all — "Microbiome Repo")

  • New curated scope gut_all (config/packs/gut.yaml): every human gut shotgun-metagenome study in the registry, all ages — 2,837 studies / 579,252 samples — curated at sample level for age at collection, sex, BMI, country, health condition (config/vocab/health_conditions.yaml), antibiotic exposure, subject and timepoint. Tables: gut_sample_determinations.parquet (one row per sample × field with route, confidence, verbatim evidence quote and source), gut_sample_metadata_wide.parquet (one row per sample, fields with route/confidence, age_category + basis, body_site_class, infant_scope), gut_studies.parquet (registry columns + per-field coverage, age-category / health-condition distributions, curated depth).
  • Routes for the 2,448 non-infant studies: R1 archive attributes (registry normalisation + a gut attribute field map of 287 keys with deterministic parsers; key triage by the utility model), R2 supplementary tables of open-access papers (exact-accession join gate, header-named columns, no model), R4 cohort-wide statements from titles / ENA descriptions / abstracts (utility model, verbatim-quote check, confidence ≤ 0.5). Health-condition text → vocabulary codes by 20 rules + a utility-model pass over 3,719 distinct (key, value) pairs. R3 (full-text prose) not run for non-infant studies.
  • The infant catalog is the infant_scope filter of this scope: its 389 studies' rows are copied verbatim (src_track infant_catalog) and win over archive-only values; infant_scope = true reproduces the infant catalog_scope (72,358 samples) exactly. The infant tables are unchanged.
  • Site: renamed Microbiome Repo; new gut/ section (coverage by age category, health conditions, countries, largest studies, DuckDB-WASM sample explorer with an "infant catalog scope" switch); home page leads with the registry → gut scope → infant scope; Methods › Curated scope gut_all; future plans (other body sites) stated on the home page.
