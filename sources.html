Sources and acknowledgements

Everything on this site is derived from public archives and open literature; this page names those sources and their terms, the software and models the pipeline uses, the funding, and the other projects that curate human-microbiome metadata — with an honest assessment of what each could add to the catalog and how it would join. Last reviewed 2026-09-28.

Development of Microbiome Repo is supported by a grant from Anthropic to the Olm Lab (University of Colorado Boulder) for organising human microbiome metadata.

Related efforts and what we could ingest

Resources that curate or harmonise human microbiome metadata. Ingestion is our assessment: used already part of the pipeline · candidate_high per-sample curated fields that join on archive accessions · candidate_medium partial overlap, cross-check or future scopes · candidate_low out of scope (amplicon) or not yet released · not_applicable derived data without per-sample metadata. Any ingested value would enter as its own evidence row (route R2, evidence_source = external.<resource>, the resource's identifier as locator) after the study's own tables in precedence, and only under terms that allow redistribution. Know one we missed? Tell us.

curatedMetagenomicData 3 (cMD 3) candidate_high Pasolli et al. 2017 Nat Methods; cMD 3 — Nature Communications 2025
Scope: human shotgun metagenomes, all body sites; manually curated per-sample metadata · Size: > 22,000 samples, 94 studies, 42 countries (cMD 3) · Licence: Artistic-2.0; metadata redistributable with attribution
Offers: Per-sample curated age, sex, BMI, country, disease, antibiotics and study condition; MetaPhlAn/HUMAnN profiles; NCBI accessions per sample.
Join: sample_metadata NCBI_accession (SRR/ERR run ids, ';'-separated) → registry runs → BioSample; study_name → PMID / BioProject.
Assessment: Best per-sample external source for the core fields; already our gold standard for the infant fields. Ingest as an external curated table (route R2, evidence_source external.cmd3, evidence_locator = cMD study_name/sample_id, confidence 0.8) with precedence after the study's own supplementary tables. Disease codes map to health_conditions.yaml by rule.
GMrepo v3 candidate_high Wu et al. 2020, Dai et al. 2022, Liu et al. 2026 NAR (v3)
Scope: human gut, 16S and shotgun; phenotype-centred curation · Size: 890 projects, 118,965 runs/samples (87,048 16S · 31,917 WGS), 302 diseases (v3) · Licence: Check the site's terms before redistribution (academic use stated).
Offers: Per-run curated phenotype (MeSH disease), age, sex, BMI, country, health status; project-level markers; RESTful API and downloads.
Join: run accession (SRR/ERR/DRR) and project id (PRJNA…) → registry runs / studies.
Assessment: The 31,917 WGS runs overlap the catalog directly; disease → MeSH gives a mapping target for health_conditions.yaml. Ingest phenotype/age/sex/BMI as external.gmrepo_v3 rows (route R2, confidence 0.75) once redistribution terms are confirmed; otherwise use only as a cross-check and link out per study.
African Human Microbiome Portal (H3ABioNet) candidate_medium Database 2024
Scope: African human microbiome samples, metadata only · Size: 14,889 records, 70 BioProjects, 72 articles · Licence: Open access (check reuse terms).
Offers: Harmonised, ontology-coded metadata (demographics, body site, disease) for African cohorts; filtered downloads.
Join: BioProject / BioSample accession.
Assessment: Improves population coverage where archive attributes are thin; join on BioProject then BioSample.
BugSigDB candidate_medium Geistlinger et al. 2023 Nat Biotechnol
Scope: curated microbial signatures from published studies, all body sites · Size: thousands of signatures from > 1,000 studies · Licence: CC-BY 4.0
Offers: Study-level curation: condition, body site, sequencing type, cohort location, linked PMID/DOI (CC-BY).
Join: PMID / DOI → registry_study_papers → study.
Assessment: Study-level condition and cohort-location annotations can support R4-like cohort statements (confidence ≤ 0.5) for studies whose papers BugSigDB curated.
HMP / iHMP data portal candidate_medium Human Microbiome Project Consortium 2012; iHMP 2019
Scope: HMP1 and iHMP cohorts (multi-site, longitudinal) · Size: > 30,000 files across the HMP programmes · Licence: Open for the public tier; controlled tier via dbGaP.
Offers: Rich subject and visit metadata for the HMP cohorts (partly controlled access).
Join: SRS / SRR accessions and BioProjects of the HMP studies.
Assessment: Per-visit metadata for the HMP gut samples in the catalog (thousands of samples); only the public tier can be redistributed.
HumanMetagenomeDB candidate_medium Kasmanas et al. 2021 NAR
Scope: human metagenomes from SRA and MG-RAST, all body sites · Size: 69,822 metagenomes, 203 standardised attributes (v1.0) · Licence: Check (UFZ).
Offers: Standardised host attributes (body site, age, sex, disease, country) from archive metadata, ontology terms.
Join: SRA sample / run accession.
Assessment: Derived from the same archive attributes as our R1, so mostly a consistency check for the 2020 snapshot; its ontology mappings (body site, disease) can validate our vocabularies.
mBodyMap candidate_medium Jin et al. 2022 NAR
Scope: human microbes across body sites with health/disease associations; per-run curated phenotypes · Size: tens of thousands of runs across 20+ body sites (see site) · Licence: Check.
Offers: Per-run body site, phenotype and disease annotation; taxon–disease associations.
Join: run accession, project id.
Assessment: Body-site and disease annotations for non-gut sites make it a candidate when the oral, skin and vaginal scopes are curated.
Meta2DB (LLNL) candidate_medium Meta2DB 2024 bioRxiv / 2026
Scope: curated shotgun metagenomic feature sets with labelled health-state metadata · Size: see Zenodo record 17315984 · Licence: Zenodo record (check).
Offers: Labelled sample metadata (health state) for shotgun studies; Centrifuge-based profiles.
Join: SRA run accession.
Assessment: Health-state labels for shotgun samples map to health_conditions.yaml; profiles out of scope.
MGnify (EBI Metagenomics) candidate_medium Richardson et al. 2023 NAR
Scope: analysed public metagenomes and amplicon studies, all biomes · Size: hundreds of thousands of analysed samples · Licence: EMBL-EBI terms (open).
Offers: Biome (GOLD) classification, ENA-linked sample metadata via the API, uniform analyses.
Join: ENA study / sample accession (native).
Assessment: Biome assignments cross-check the registry's body-site classification for the studies MGnify analysed.
MicrobeAtlas candidate_medium Cell 2026
Scope: all microbiome samples in SRA (amplicon and metagenomic), all biomes · Size: 2,390,937 samples, 52,950 studies · Licence: Check.
Offers: Harmonised SSU-rRNA community profiles, extracted metadata keywords, environmental ontology and geographic coordinates per sample.
Join: SRA sample / run accession.
Assessment: Geography and environment annotations could fill country for samples without a geo attribute (as an inferred route with low confidence) and cross-check body-site classes; the profiles are rRNA-based and outside our scope.
MicrobiomeDB (VEuPathDB) candidate_medium Oliveira et al. 2018 NAR
Scope: curated microbiome studies (mostly 16S, some shotgun), human and other hosts · Size: dozens of studies with rich sample metadata · Licence: Check (VEuPathDB data-use policy).
Offers: Deeply curated per-sample metadata with ontology terms, downloadable per study.
Join: SRA accessions where deposited.
Assessment: Small overlap but very high metadata quality for the studies it covers; worth an accession-level join test.
EMBERS (LLM extraction of metadata from 26,435 gut-microbiome papers) candidate_low bioRxiv 2024
Scope: paper-derived sample metadata for human gut studies · Size: 26,435 papers · Licence: Check (preprint; data release status unknown).
Offers: Automated extraction and harmonisation of sample metadata from papers — the same idea as our R3/R4 routes.
Join: PMID → study; sample-level join depends on the released tables.
Assessment: Methodologically closest to our approach; ingest only if a table with accessions and verbatim evidence is released.
Human Microbiome Compendium (MicroBioMap) candidate_low Abdill et al. 2023 bioRxiv
Scope: human gut 16S amplicon samples from SRA · Size: ≈ 168,000 samples · Licence: CC-BY
Offers: Uniformly processed 16S profiles and a harmonised sample_metadata.tsv (CC-BY).
Join: SRA run / sample accession.
Assessment: Amplicon-only, so outside the shotgun scope; its harmonised metadata columns and the study list are a useful comparison for the registry's amplicon_misfiled calls.
PRIME candidate_low 2025
Scope: human 16S amplicon studies with harmonised phenotypes · Size: 53,449 samples, 111 studies, 93 body sites · Licence: Check.
Offers: Harmonised disease, demographics, body-site and protocol metadata for amplicon studies.
Join: SRA accessions.
Assessment: Amplicon-only; useful as a vocabulary reference for phenotype categories.
gutMEGA, gutMDisorder, Disbiome, HMDAD, GutMetaNet, HGMT not_applicable various (2020–2025)
Scope: derived quantitative or literature-curated gut microbiome resources · Size: — · Licence: various
Offers: Taxon–condition associations and processed abundances rather than per-sample archive-linked metadata.
Join: PMID where given.
Assessment: No per-sample metadata to ingest; listed for completeness. HGMT (tumour cohorts, 18,630 stool samples) could supply cohort-level condition labels for immunotherapy studies.
