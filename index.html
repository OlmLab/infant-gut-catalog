Microbiome Repo

Every public human shotgun-metagenome study in ENA/SRA/DDBJ (registry) and a curated, evidence-linked catalog of human gut metagenomes of all ages. Two tiers: the registry lists every public ENA study with a human shotgun-metagenome signal — 54,410 studies, 1,984,314 sequencing runs, all body sites and ages — each classified from the archive record (host, UBERON-anchored body sites, life stages, assay, access) and, where harvested, with per-BioSample site / age / sex / country. The catalog is the curated tier: every registry study of the human gut, all ages — 2,837 studies, 579,252 samples — with per-sample age, sex, BMI, country, health condition, antibiotic exposure, subject and timepoint recovered from archive attributes, supplementary tables and papers, every value carrying a verbatim evidence quote, a route (R1–R4) and a confidence. Future plans: the same curation for the other human microbiomes in the registry — oral, skin, vaginal / urogenital, respiratory, milk — one scope at a time.
