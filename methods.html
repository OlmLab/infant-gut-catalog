Methods and caveats

Condensed from the release reports; counts below are recomputed from the release tables at build time, method descriptions and audit statistics are taken from the reports. All documents: README · Data dictionary · Changelog · Sandpiper report · Authors report · Data-model fix report · Catalog report · Extraction report.

Age scope (v1.2). "Infant-scope" on this site means age evidence: age_scope ∈ {infant_evidenced, study_all_infant} (74,695 samples). The older body-site definition (primary or unknown site; 146,336 samples) is kept as the denominator of the package coverage summary. 61 included studies are mixed-age deposits. role is shown as evidenced and is unknown when unevidenced.

Scope

DNA shotgun metagenomics (ENA library_source=METAGENOMIC, library_strategy WGS/WXS; OTHER/Targeted-Capture adjudicated case by case), human subjects aged 0–36 months (preterm included), gut/stool as the primary site. Maternal stool, breast milk and vaginal samples are retained only when they sit inside an infant cohort; they are labelled by body_site_class and role. Every committed value carries a labelled verbatim evidence quote (≤12 words) and passes the project's curation-rule validators; no identifier was written from model memory.

Universe enumeration

9,579 candidate studies were enumerated from ENA in several rounds: the original taxon-framed enumeration, gap-fill re-enumeration after two taxid bugs were found, growth channels (virome slice, misfiled GENOMIC deposits, literature snowball, external repositories), a frame-free sweep of every METAGENOMIC WGS/WXS run with a human signal, and a BioSample-attribute / cohort-name / reanalysis-link round. Non-INSDC archives (GSA, KoNA, CNGBdb) were assessed and kept as a list-only annex. Final status: excluded 9,138 · included 389 · human_review 52. The Universe page lists every study with its reason code.

Triage cascade

A study is included iff four criteria hold with a cited quote: (1) human host; (2) at least one shotgun metagenomic run; (3) primary site gut/stool/meconium; (4) at least one sample from a subject aged 0–1,100 days, evidenced by an archive attribute, sample-title convention, study description, linked paper text, or a per-sample supplementary table whose IDs map to the study's own accessions. The cascade ran deterministic auto-exclusion (non-human host, isolate genomes, amplicon/RNA-only, environmental/synthetic), a batched screen, a four-criterion rubric with replicated judgements, adjudication of disagreements with paper context, a deterministic supplement-table rescue, and two rounds of full-text review of the undecidable residue. Reported validation (CATALOG_REPORT §1, §4, §8): study-level recall on the 22 curatedMetagenomicData infant BioProjects: 17/22 by the automated cascade, 18/22 after the human-review pass (one study, decision_stage = session_model_review) (0.972 sample-weighted); gold-negative include rate 2/108; inter-judge κ 0.77 (3-class) / 0.85 (include vs not) on 200 blind re-judgements; capture–recapture coverage of in-universe infant shotgun studies 0.77–0.98 (0.78 conservative).

Literature and cohorts

Europe PMC abstracts were screened for human + infant + shotgun + primary-data papers, full texts were fetched as JATS, and accession mentions in data-availability sections and text were resolved to BioProjects (deterministic where a section names a single accession, model-adjudicated otherwise). The release carries 973 study↔paper links over 411 papers. Studies and papers sharing subjects were clustered into 373 cohorts; unique-infant estimates are quoted from an index publication where one exists.

Taxonomy: Sandpiper / SingleM community profiles (GTDB R232)

Method. Community profiles come from Sandpiper 2.0.0 (Woodcroft, B. J. et al. Comprehensive taxonomic identification of microbial species in metagenomic data using SingleM and Sandpiper. Nature Biotechnology, 2025), which runs SingleM on public metagenomes keyed by run accession. The bulk snapshot is Zenodo record 20419175 (CC-BY), taxonomy GTDB R232 on every row; GlobDB profiles are never mixed in. 87,125 of 174,022 catalog runs and 79,473 of 154,206 samples (52%) in 304 studies are profiled (a study counts as profiled when at least one of its sample rows is profiled; 302 studies have profiled runs registered under their own accession, and 181 have enough catalog-scope samples of adequate depth for a composition panel). Profiles are Sandpiper 2.0.0 outputs taken from Zenodo snapshot 20419175; the linked live Sandpiper run pages may show a newer Sandpiper version. Every profiled sample and every run links to https://sandpiper.qut.edu.au/run/<run>.

Aggregation rule. Profiles are per run; the catalog unit is the BioSample (or the run for the six run-keyed deposits). For a sample with several profiled runs the filled coverage per taxon is summed across runs and then normalised — equivalent to profiling the concatenated reads (up to SingleM's per-run 0.35× noise floor). Relative abundances are never averaged across runs. Relative abundance at any rank = filled coverage ÷ root (Bacteria + Archaea); an explicit unassigned_at_<rank> bin carries coverage that stopped above that rank, so the novel/unclassified fraction is never renormalised away. Study panels show the mean over profiled catalog-scope samples (age-scope infant AND gut/unknown body site) with adequate depth; each panel states n and the age-scope breakdown of every profiled sample in the study. Values are coverage-based (≈ cell proportions), prokaryotes only — not comparable with MetaPhlAn read fractions or 16S. 2,457 profiled samples have unprofiled runs (sp_partial); 99 multi-run samples have discordant run profiles (Bray–Curtis > 0.5; human-review hint only — most are VLP + bulk library pairs).

Indicator taxa are defined in GTDB terms. sp_ra_g_Bifidobacterium = GTDB g__Bifidobacterium (stable). sp_ra_f_Bacteroidaceae includes Bacteroides and Phocaeicola (GTDB moved B. vulgatus/dorei/plebeius/coprocola to Phocaeicola; the NCBI-sense Bacteroides ≈ this family; the GTDB genus alone is about half). sp_ra_enterobacterales_core = sum of six GTDB genera Escherichia, Klebsiella, Enterobacter, Citrobacter, Salmonella, Serratia (GTDB f__Enterobacteriaceae absorbed Vibrionaceae, Pasteurellaceae and others, so the family is not used). sp_ra_f_Lactobacillaceae captures the GTDB split of Lactobacillus; Clostridioides is C. difficile's GTDB genus. Shannon diversity is computed over genus bins including the unassigned bin as one category.

QC flags — what they may and may not decide. Sandpiper's own flags exist only in its per-run API, so they were reproduced deterministically from Sandpiper's published definitions on each run's organism and library labels; the complete flag vocabulary used on this site is the table below (single source: sandpiper_flag_table.csv). Low complexity (6,029 profiled samples) fires on Bifidobacterium-/Enterobacterales-dominated neonatal stool — the expected state of preterm and newborn samples — and is display-only, never a triage signal. Low depth (root coverage < 2×; 460 samples) hides the bars. Low prokaryotic read fraction (SPF; median 87.16%) is expected for meconium and early NICU stool and is a depth/quality label, not an exclusion. Known-species fraction (median 90.54%) below 50 % at high depth is a mislabelled-site hint routed to auditor review. Non-human-host / named-microbe / synthetic / RNA rules are legitimate triage hints and are listed per study as sp_frac_runs_flagged and sp_auditor_attention; no verdict was changed automatically. Composition was never used to assign an age or any other metadata value.

What is on the site and what is not. On site: per-sample summary columns (sp_*) in the wide table, the top-15 genera per sample (loaded on demand in the explorer), per-study mean phylum/genus panels, per-run QC for all runs. Off site (GitHub Release assets): the full sample × rank × taxon profiles (10.2 M rows, ≈100 MB) and the per-run profiles as delivered. Decision and value history

Every study page carries a collapsible Decision history table (32,231 stage verdicts over all screened studies, from study_verdict_history.parquet) and the explorer's detail panel shows the Value history of a sample (45,886 superseded, rejected, dropped or moved determinations, from value_history.parquet, each with its reason and what replaced it). Where a verdict or value changed, both rows are visible.

Flag or confirm a value

Study pages, cohort pages and the explorer detail panel carry Flag an issue and Confirm correct buttons. They open a prefilled GitHub issue (template catalog-finding.yml, label finding) with the identifier, field, current value, route, confidence, evidence quote, release tag and page. A Curator session converts labelled issues into audit/findings/*.csv rows, re-validates them with the curation-rule validators and applies them with decision_stage = auditor_review; the change then appears in the value history. Confirmations accumulate into a human-verified truth set that will be reported per field and tier next to the cMD numbers.

Coverage

Per-study coverage is bimodal — a field is recoverable for essentially every sample of a study or for none — so recoverability tiers predicted before extraction are shown on each study page. Field-level counts are in Fields; the figure on the home page summarises them.

Limitations

  • Age is recoverable for about 43 % of infant-scope samples. Roughly 42k samples sit in studies whose per-sample supplementary tables are keyed by identifiers absent from the archive (e.g. TEDDY) and can only be joined with submitter help.
  • Held-out gold covers age, delivery, feeding, preterm, antibiotics and gestational age only; probiotic, HMO, NEC, birth weight and maternal antibiotics have no external truth set.
  • R3 read at most two papers per study; multi-paper cohorts were partially read (token budget). R4 rests on abstracts/descriptions for studies without open-access full text.
  • Mother–infant linking is partial; many studies expose no subject identifier, so n_unique_infants_est is an estimate.
  • 9,559 samples in included studies carry a resolved age > 1,100 d (mothers, adult cohorts with an infant subgroup) and are flagged with adult_age_flag; the triggering value is a determination row with parse_note out_of_scope_adult.
  • Sandpiper profiles cover 52% of samples; ENA-only ERR/DRR deposits, controlled-access and very recent runs are under-represented (see Taxonomy).
  • Author names are string-matched and undisambiguated; 103 included studies have no author on record.
  • Extension fields (health_condition, multiple_birth, sibling_in_study, geo_subregion) were added in a single pass and are less audited than the core fields.
  • Study-level recall gate (≥0.95) was not met (0.77); the sample-weighted gate was (0.97). Recent, weakly annotated deposits are the soft spot.

Compute and token budget

Built 2026-09-17 → 2026-09-25 with Claude-model pipelines (Haiku for screening/normalisation, Sonnet for rubric judgement and prose extraction, Opus for adjudication and audits) plus deterministic parsers. The reports record ≈44.9 M LLM tokens for the whole pipeline, of which ≈14.0 M for the per-sample extraction phase and ≈2.7 M for recovery round 2 (EXTRACTION_REPORT §5, §8).
