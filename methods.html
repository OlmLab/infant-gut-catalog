Methods and caveats

Condensed from the two release reports: CATALOG_REPORT (universe, triage, coverage) and EXTRACTION_REPORT (per-sample metadata). Counts below are recomputed from the release tables; method descriptions and audit statistics are taken from the reports.

Scope

DNA shotgun metagenomics (ENA library_source=METAGENOMIC, library_strategy WGS/WXS; OTHER/Targeted-Capture adjudicated case by case), human subjects aged 0–36 months (preterm included), gut/stool as the primary site. Maternal stool, breast milk and vaginal samples are retained only when they sit inside an infant cohort; they are labelled by body_site_class and role. Every committed value carries a labelled verbatim evidence quote (≤12 words) and passes the project's curation-rule validators; no identifier was written from model memory.

Universe enumeration

9,579 candidate studies were enumerated from ENA in several rounds: the original taxon-framed enumeration, gap-fill re-enumeration after two taxid bugs were found, growth channels (virome slice, misfiled GENOMIC deposits, literature snowball, external repositories), a frame-free sweep of every METAGENOMIC WGS/WXS run with a human signal, and a BioSample-attribute / cohort-name / reanalysis-link round. Non-INSDC archives (GSA, KoNA, CNGBdb) were assessed and kept as a list-only annex. Final status: excluded 9,138 · included 389 · human_review 52. The Universe page lists every study with its reason code.

Triage cascade

A study is included iff four criteria hold with a cited quote: (1) human host; (2) at least one shotgun metagenomic run; (3) primary site gut/stool/meconium; (4) at least one sample from a subject aged 0–1,100 days, evidenced by an archive attribute, sample-title convention, study description, linked paper text, or a per-sample supplementary table whose IDs map to the study's own accessions. The cascade ran deterministic auto-exclusion (non-human host, isolate genomes, amplicon/RNA-only, environmental/synthetic), a batched screen, a four-criterion rubric with replicated judgements, adjudication of disagreements with paper context, a deterministic supplement-table rescue, and two rounds of full-text review of the undecidable residue. Reported validation (CATALOG_REPORT §1, §4, §8): study-level recall against the curatedMetagenomicData gold set 17/22 (0.972 sample-weighted); gold-negative include rate 2/108; inter-judge κ 0.77 (3-class) / 0.85 (include vs not) on 200 blind re-judgements; capture–recapture coverage of in-universe infant shotgun studies 0.77–0.98 (0.78 conservative).

Literature and cohorts

Europe PMC abstracts were screened for human + infant + shotgun + primary-data papers, full texts were fetched as JATS, and accession mentions in data-availability sections and text were resolved to BioProjects (deterministic where a section names a single accession, model-adjudicated otherwise). The release carries 972 study↔paper links over 410 papers. Studies and papers sharing subjects were clustered into 373 cohorts; unique-infant estimates are quoted from an index publication where one exists.

Precedence. One value per sample × field: R1 > R2 > R3 > R4, then narrower scope, then higher confidence. Cross-route disagreements were adjudicated once per conflict pattern; group statements had to pass a consistency filter against per-sample values and every distinct group statement was audited with a failure-mode checklist (eligibility criteria read as observations, "healthy" read as term, timepoint lists read as one age, care setting read as exposure). Rows that fail a validator are not in the release tables. A second recovery round added ID-mapping rescue for supplementary tables, PDF/DOCX supplements, unit resolution for unitless ages, multi-paper cohorts, deterministic subject propagation of per-infant-constant fields and date arithmetic (collection − birth date).

Coverage

Per-study coverage is bimodal — a field is recoverable for essentially every sample of a study or for none — so recoverability tiers predicted before extraction are shown on each study page. Field-level counts are in Fields; the figure on the home page summarises them.

Limitations

  • Age is recoverable for about 37 % of infant-scope samples. Roughly 42k samples sit in studies whose per-sample supplementary tables are keyed by identifiers absent from the archive (e.g. TEDDY) and can only be joined with submitter help.
  • Held-out gold covers age, delivery, feeding, preterm, antibiotics and gestational age only; probiotic, HMO, NEC, birth weight and maternal antibiotics have no external truth set.
  • R3 read at most two papers per study; multi-paper cohorts were partially read (token budget). R4 rests on abstracts/descriptions for studies without open-access full text.
  • Mother–infant linking is partial; many studies expose no subject identifier, so n_unique_infants_est is an estimate.
  • ~9,567 samples in included studies carry adult ages (mothers, adult cohorts with an infant subgroup) and are flagged with adult_age_flag.
  • Extension fields (health_condition, multiple_birth, sibling_in_study, geo_subregion) were added in a single pass and are less audited than the core fields.
  • Study-level recall gate (≥0.95) was not met (0.77); the sample-weighted gate was (0.97). Recent, weakly annotated deposits are the soft spot.

Compute and token budget

Built 2026-09-17 → 2026-09-25 with Claude-model pipelines (Haiku for screening/normalisation, Sonnet for rubric judgement and prose extraction, Opus for adjudication and audits) plus deterministic parsers. The reports record ≈44.9 M LLM tokens for the whole pipeline, of which ≈14.0 M for the per-sample extraction phase and ≈2.7 M for recovery round 2 (EXTRACTION_REPORT §5, §8).
