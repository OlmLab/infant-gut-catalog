Curated scope: human gut, all ages

Every study in the registry whose runs include human gut / stool shotgun metagenomes — 2,837 studies, 579,252 samples — curated at sample level for the fields of config/packs/gut.yaml: age at collection, sex, BMI, country, health condition, antibiotic exposure, subject and timepoint. Values come from four routes with decreasing precedence: R1 the sample's own archive attributes, R2 supplementary tables of the linked open-access papers joined on exact accessions, R3 paper prose, R4 abstract / study description (cohort-wide statements, confidence ≤ 0.5). Every value carries a verbatim quote, a labelled source and a confidence in gut_sample_determinations.parquet.

The infant gut catalog is this scope filtered to its infant category: infant_scope = true (72,358 samples = the infant catalog's catalog-scope count) reproduces it exactly; the 389 infant studies keep their deeper R1–R4 curation (source infant_catalog). Non-infant samples were curated in releases R2026.7–R2026.8 (source gut_all_v1): R1 for every sample, R2 where an open-access paper had a joinable supplementary table, R3 cohort-wide statements from the Methods / Results of the first open-access paper (two independent Sonnet-class replicates must agree; confidence ≤ 0.7), R4 from abstracts — see Methods and the depth column of the study table.
